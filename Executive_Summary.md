# Executive Summary

The Real-Time Ferry Ticket Sales & Redemption Analytics project provides an interactive analytics platform for monitoring passenger movement between Toronto mainland and Toronto Island Park.

The project processes ticket sales and redemption records collected every fifteen minutes. Through data preprocessing, feature engineering, exploratory data analysis, and visualization, the dashboard identifies peak travel hours, seasonal demand, and passenger flow.

The dashboard enables operations teams to make data-driven decisions regarding ferry scheduling, staffing, and congestion management.

Key benefits include:

- Improved ferry scheduling
- Reduced waiting time
- Better resource allocation
- Enhanced passenger experience
- Data-driven policy planning
